The short version

I'm a UI, WordPress and HTML email developer based in Bangalore, India. At Webenza India Pvt Ltd, Bangalore (Jan 2022 – Apr 2025) I took client websites from creative handoff through to launch, then kept them running with updates and fixes. I also design and automate email campaigns in Mailchimp.
